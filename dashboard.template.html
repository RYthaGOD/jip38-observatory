Has Jito burned the JTO it promised to burn?

JIP-38 commits 80% of JTX platform fees to the DAO, and 100% of that share to open-market JTO buybacks and permanent burns, through Q4 2027. Every step settles on chain, so it can be checked rather than taken on trust. This page is that check, derived from Solana directly.

The bar is the measured ratio. The vertical rule is what JIP-38 promises.

This is not evidence of a breach. JIP-38 runs for at least a year, through Q4 2027, and a programme that batches its burns has broken no promise two months in. What can be said is narrower: with roughly $159,000 of fee revenue committed to buying and burning JTO, none has yet been burned — and against a public position that holders can follow “fee collection, purchases, and burns in real time”, there is as yet nothing to follow on the burn side.

Why the burn total is checkable at all

Four facts from chain. Together they make the burned quantity an exact figure rather than an estimate.

01
Exactly 1,000,000,000 JTO was ever minted chain A single mintToChecked on 2023-11-27, five minutes after the mint was created.
02
Minting was closed permanently on 2023-12-04 chain The authority set mintTokens to null. Across all 239 of its transactions that is its only supply-changing act. No JTO can ever be created again.
03
So supply can fall by burning, and by nothing else chain Which makes genesis minus current supply an exact quantity destroyed — no enumeration required, and no index to trust.
04
JTO has been destroyed since the token existed chain But that is roughly 55× more than JIP-38 could have produced from the fee revenue reported so far, so it predates activation and is outside this commitment.
